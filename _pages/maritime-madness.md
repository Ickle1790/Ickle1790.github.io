Maritime Madness is a multiplayer tabletop board game (ideally 3-4 players) where you play as pirates, navigating their ships through the ocean to reach an island of treasure by rolling dice, and moving across the board based on the number shown.
Certain tiles on the board are different than the rest, as when they are activated, unique effects come into play, from receiving coins to having to move back down the board to give your opponents an advantage.

{% include gallery id="gallery_maritime" layout="third" thumb_height="180px" %}
(The pictures above were taken with the consent of those involved.)

I made the game as part of a solo project for my Games Design and Development course. I designed the concept for the game as well as a majority of the physical assets, from the boards to the rule sheet, as well as the many types of cards available. The plastic coins and compass needed were borrowed from my university lecturer, and the dice used were stylised dice purchased online.
