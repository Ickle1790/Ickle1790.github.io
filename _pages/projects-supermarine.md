My 2D Game, "Super Marine's Super Showdown!", is a game developed by me, where you play as the titular Super Marine as he runs through an alien planet. The main goal of the game is to make your way across various platforming challenges, from pushing objects to create a path to navigating moving platforms, and finding keys to open doors to make your way to the boss at the end of the stage.

You can use your Assault Rifle or Shotgun to attack multiple enemy types, from the swaying tentacles emerging from the ground to alien soldiers with golden energy rifles patrolling the field.
If an enemy physically attacks you or shoots you with an energy blast from their gun, they deplete your health from the green healthbar at the top of the screen, which can be restored once heart pickups are collected.

{% include gallery id="gallery_marine" layout="third" thumb_height="180px" %}

Unlike other games where you may simply swap weapons via a button input or a select wheel, in "Super Marine's Super Showdown!", you must reach floating boxes marked with different ammo types (orange for Assault Rifle, red for Shotgun) which, when jumped into, change the ammunition type stored within your bullet.
This is intended to force players to strategise and plan ahead - should you swap to a Shotgun while you have the chance to deal more damage to enemies but risk missing shots more frequently while on moving platforms, or keep your Assault Rifle to have a steady aim but deal less damage to enemies and breakable walls for quicker gameplay?

{% include gallery id="gallery_marine2" layout="third" thumb_height="180px" %}

This game was designed by me as a solo project for my Games Design and Development course. It was made in Unity, with code written in Visual Studio Code, with visual assets created by me in Aseprite, as well as sourced from free external art sources.
